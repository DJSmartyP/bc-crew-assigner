Interstellar Deployment Planner – cross-device organiser sign-in fix

Replace app.js in the GitHub repo.

What was wrong:
The different-device flow correctly asked the organiser to confirm the email address,
and Firebase signed them in, but the app had already exited its normal startup flow.
That meant the dashboard did not open.

What changed:
After successful email confirmation, the app reloads once with the authenticated
Firebase session. The normal organiser dashboard then opens.

No Firebase settings or Firestore rules need changing for this fix.
