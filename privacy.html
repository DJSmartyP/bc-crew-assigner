Privacy Notice

Interstellar Deployment Planner · Last updated 1 September 2026

Controller: Nick Smith-Patel
Privacy contact: nickpatel.trainer@gmail.com

1. What this notice covers

This notice explains how personal information is used when you use Interstellar Deployment Planner (the “Planner”). The Planner is a crew-allocation tool that lets organisers create deployments, collect player preferences, calculate suggested crew assignments and produce crew manifests.

The Planner is designed for low-risk game and event planning. Please do not enter sensitive or special-category information such as health information, political or religious beliefs, financial information, government identifiers, passwords or other information that is not needed to allocate a crew position.

2. Information collected

Organisers

  • Google account display name and email address;
  • Firebase user identifier and authentication-provider information;
  • deployments created or managed by the organiser;
  • administrative actions such as crew locks, assignment overrides and deployment settings.

Players

  • the name or callsign entered for a deployment;
  • ship preference where a deployment has more than one ship;
  • ranked station preferences or a “No preference / fill a gap” choice;
  • stations marked as roles the player would really prefer not to use;
  • submission and update timestamps;
  • suggested or organiser-fixed ship and station assignments;
  • anonymous Firebase identifiers and local browser identifiers used to distinguish records and allow a returning player on the same device to reopen their own preferences.

Technical information

The service and its infrastructure providers may process ordinary technical information needed to deliver the site, such as IP address, browser/device information, security events, request logs and error information.

3. Why the information is used

Personal information is used to:

  • authenticate organisers and administer organiser accounts;
  • create and manage deployments;
  • collect and update crew preferences;
  • prevent duplicate player names within the same deployment and allow locally owned records to be reopened;
  • calculate suggested ship and station assignments;
  • allow organisers to review, lock or override assignments;
  • generate crew manifests;
  • protect the service from misuse and troubleshoot faults.

4. Lawful basis

The principal lawful basis is legitimate interests. The legitimate interests are providing and securing a practical deployment-planning service, enabling organisers to allocate participants to crew roles, preventing duplicate registrations and maintaining the integrity of deployments.

The Planner uses a limited amount of ordinary personal information for a low-impact purpose. It is not intended to process sensitive information or to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Automated crew suggestions

The Planner uses an automated allocation algorithm to suggest crew positions based on player preferences, available places, ship settings, response order as a final tie-break and organiser locks. These suggestions are part of a recreational planning tool. They do not have legal or similarly significant effects, and organisers can review or override the suggested crew.

6. Who can see information

The organiser responsible for a deployment and the Planner administrator can see the deployment and the player information needed to manage it. Participants may see crew names/callsigns and current suggested assignments shown on the deployment roster.

Detailed player preferences are intended for organiser/administrator use in the interface. However, the current client-side architecture calculates allocations in the browser, so authenticated participants may technically receive deployment/player data needed for that calculation. For that reason, do not submit sensitive information to the Planner.

7. Service providers and external resources

The Planner relies on third-party infrastructure and resources, including:

  • Google / Firebase for authentication and database services;
  • GitHub Pages for hosting the static website;
  • web-font, JavaScript and image/CDN providers used to display interface assets.

Those providers may process technical connection information under their own terms and privacy arrangements. Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for international transfers, the relevant provider transfer mechanisms and contractual safeguards apply.

8. Browser storage and cookies

The Planner uses browser storage and authentication technologies that are necessary to provide sign-in, keep sessions working, distinguish locally registered players and reopen a player’s own record on the same device. The Planner does not intentionally use advertising cookies or optional analytics cookies at present.

Clearing browser/site data may remove the Planner’s ability to recognise a locally owned player record on that device.

9. How long information is kept

There is currently no automatic time-based deletion process. Deployment and player information is kept until the relevant deployment is deleted by an organiser or administrator, or until deletion is requested and can lawfully be carried out.

Organiser profile information is kept while the organiser account is active. Where organiser access is removed, limited account information may be retained so that access remains blocked and, where appropriate, can be restored. Local browser information remains until it is cleared, overwritten or removed by the user/browser.

10. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to request access to your personal information, correction, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability.

Your right to object: where processing relies on legitimate interests, you have the right to object to that processing. The circumstances will determine whether the processing must stop.

To exercise a right or ask a privacy question, email nickpatel.trainer@gmail.com. You may be asked for enough information to identify the relevant deployment or record.

11. Complaints

If you have a concern, please contact Nick Smith-Patel first so it can be investigated. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). Information about making a complaint is available at ico.org.uk.

12. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the Planner changes, when new service providers are introduced or when data-protection requirements change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest published version.
